🧭 Three Distinct Pillars of Placement Intelligence
To ensure algorithmic honesty and clarity, this platform rigorously separates statistical estimates, transparent readiness scores, and preparation guidance:
1. Demo Model Estimate
A supervised machine learning classification estimate trained on synthetic historical placement profiles. Expressed as a whole percentage; not validated for actual LPU students.
2. Student Readiness Score
A deterministic 0-100 score calculated via transparent university weights across 5 pillars (Technical, Aptitude, Communication, Projects, Internships) mapped to 3 canonical tiers.
3. Actionable Preparation Plan
Prescriptive 30-, 60-, and 90-day sprint milestones addressing specific competency deficits. Guidance based on gap analysis, not guaranteed placement.

🌟 Strong preparation (Score ≥ 70): Consistent academic standing, solid algorithmic problem solving (120+ DSA solved), zero/one backlog, and confident communication. Ready for campus placement drives.
📈 Developing (40 ≤ Score < 70): Demonstrates foundational syntax, core CS concepts, and basic aptitude, but requires focused milestone preparation in system design and mock interviews.
⚠️ Needs attention (Score < 40): Identified gaps in core technical courses, aptitude thresholds, or multiple active backlogs (≥2). Primary focus is academic recovery and structured coding fundamentals.

Quantifies which student attributes have the highest overall influence on supervised readiness predictions (Gini Impurity Metric; Gender excluded).
📌 Model Audit Investigation: Why Active Backlogs Ranks Prominently (~12.2% Relative Importance)

In both the synthetic student data generator and binary classification training labels, students carrying 2 or more active backlogs are subject to a strict clearance cap (maximum 45% placement probability) and excluded from the placed binary label. Because backlogs act as an uncompromisable recruitment gatekeeper across IT recruiters, tree-based models split on this feature near their root nodes to achieve high initial information gain.

Note: Features are plotted in strictly descending order of relative importance percentage. Gini impurity measures the total reduction in node impurity brought by each feature over all decision trees.
